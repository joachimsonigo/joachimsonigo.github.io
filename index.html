About · Profile

My background is in aerospace engineering. Today I work in satellite product assurance.

At OHB Sweden, I support satellite programmes through verification planning, requirements traceability and test coordination. I am product assurance manager on the H2020 IOD/IOV EIS programme and co-product assurance manager on the EPS STERNA constellation.

Before that, I worked on aircraft design at Air France and process improvement at Thermo Fisher Scientific. I studied aerospace engineering at IPSA, with an exchange at the University of Limerick.
