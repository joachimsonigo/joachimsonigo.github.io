InnoSat — Product Assurance

Product assurance across OHB Sweden's InnoSat product line: verification planning, requirements traceability and test coordination.

Team
30
Role
Product Assurance Engineer
Duration
Sep 2025 — 2026
Tools
ECSS · SPENVIS · Internal tools

Overview

InnoSat is OHB Sweden's small-satellite product line. I joined as a Product Assurance Engineer in September 2025 and worked across the product line before moving into a product assurance manager role in 2026.

Developing a standard platform such as the Innosat requires careful requirement management and traceability to ensure consistency across different missions.

My work

  • Defined verification plans and test strategies, and coordinated test activities.
  • Maintained traceability between requirements and verification evidence.
  • Worked with project teams and suppliers on quality activities and verification reporting.
  • Developed internal workflow tools and databases, including lessons-learned management and a Python-based acronym reference.

Approach

Make assurance work clear and traceable: requirements are linked to evidence, and process information is turned into tools the team actually uses.

There was a need to have a fully implemented Lessons Learned system to capture and share knowledge across the product line that wasn't in an Excel file. I developed a solution in Microsoft SharePoint making use of the copilot agents.

Outcome

The Lessons Learned system has been successfully implemented and is now being used by the team to capture and share knowledge across different missions.

Some programme and internal details are not public. This page describes the work at a high level and does not disclose confidential information.
