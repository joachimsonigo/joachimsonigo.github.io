OHB Sweden · Satellite product assurance

InnoSat

Supporting confidence in satellite programmes through product assurance, verification planning and clear requirements traceability.

Role & scope

Joined OHB Sweden as a Product Assurance Engineer in 2025 and progressed to Product Assurance Manager in 2026.

Programme responsibilities include product assurance management on H2020 IOD/IOV EIS and co-management on the EPS STERNA satellite constellation project.

Focus areas

  • Verification planning
  • Requirements traceability
  • Test coordination
  • Quality processes
  • Continuous improvement

What the work involves

Turning project requirements and quality objectives into coordinated, reviewable engineering work.

Plan and verify

Define verification plans and test strategies, coordinate test activities, and maintain traceability between requirements and evidence.

Coordinate quality

Work with project teams and suppliers on quality activities, records and clear verification reporting.

Improve processes

Develop internal tools and databases that support document and change workflows, lessons learned and shared reference information.

Make information useful

Apply software and data tools, including Node.js, Microsoft 365 and Python, to improve access to process information.

A practical, evidence-led approach

The aim is to make assurance activities clear and traceable throughout a project, helping teams organise verification work and maintain useful records.

Some programme and internal process details are not public. This overview intentionally describes the work at a high level and does not disclose confidential technical information.
