Overview

The H2020 IOD/IOV EIS programme is an OHB Sweden mission for in-orbit demonstration and validation. As product assurance manager, I am responsible for product assurance on the programme.

The mission focuses on demonstrating and validating new technologies for Earth observation.

My work

  • Product Assurance
  • Supplier Coordination
  • Review and Documentation

Outcome

Product assurance has supported the satellite through its development, keeping quality records and reviews in order for the mission’s next phases.

Some programme and internal details are not public. This page describes the work at a high level and does not disclose confidential information.
