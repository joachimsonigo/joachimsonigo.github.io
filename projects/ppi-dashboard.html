Practical Process Improvement Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard supporting Practical Process Improvement in Thermo Fisher Scientific's Immuno Diagnostics Division.

Team
6
Role
Master Student, PPI Business System
Duration
Feb 2025 — Aug 2025
Tools
Power BI · Dataverse · Python · Internal tools

Overview

During my master thesis at Thermo Fisher Scientific, I developed a Power BI dashboard to support Practical Process Improvement (PPI) in the Immuno Diagnostics Division.

Process-improvement results were tracked in different places, which made their overall impact hard to see. I led the adoption of a new internal tool and developed a Power BI dashboard that gave employees across the division visibility into the impact of their improvements.

My work

  • Connected several data sources to bring process-improvement data together in one place.
  • Designed and implemented KPIs and visualizations to provide insights into process improvement initiatives and their impact.
  • Led the rollout of the dashboard across sites, supporting teams as they adopted it.

Approach

We highlighted how each department's goals could be seen and tracked through the dashboard.

Outcome

The dashboard was adopted across the division, giving teams better visibility of process-improvement work and supporting decision-making.

Some programme and internal details are not public. This page describes the work at a high level and does not disclose confidential information.
