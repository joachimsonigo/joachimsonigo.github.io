Overview

EPS STERNA is a satellite constellation project contracted to OHB Sweden. As co-product assurance manager, I share responsibility for making sure the product is designed, built and verified to meet its quality and reliability requirements.

I work closely with the instrument supplier in order to ensure that the product meets the required specifications and quality standards.

My work

  • Product assurance for the instrument, working directly with its supplier.
  • Organise and chair supplier qualification reviews (EQSRs): collecting review comments (RIDs), consolidating them and writing the review report.
  • Coordinate product assurance activities between the supplier and the OHB project team.

Approach

Product assurance on the supplier’s equipment is shared between specialists, so each unit gets focused attention. I keep reviews structured and traceable, from the first review comment through to the final report.

Outcome

Supporting the programme through its equipment qualification reviews, with clear records of how each review comment is resolved.

Some programme and internal details are not public. This page describes the work at a high level and does not disclose confidential information.
